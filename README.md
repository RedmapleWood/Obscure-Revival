<h1 align="center">🔦 Obscure Revival</h1>
<p align="center">
  <em>"Not all brilliance is born to shine. Some only need a new stage."</em>
</p>
<p align="center">
  <strong>Obscure Revival</strong> is a Minecraft mod dedicated to unearthing forgotten, overlooked, and underutilized elements of the game—and giving them a stage to truly shine.
</p>

---

## ✨ Core Philosophy: Crowning the Overlooked

This isn’t a mod about adding more for the sake of more. It’s a **revival movement**—focused on breathing new life into what already exists.

### ⚔️ Chainmail Armor Redux *(Implemented)*
Chainmail armor now specializes in **slash-deflection defense**. Its protection value **grows dynamically** the longer you survive a fight—rewarding endurance over brute force.

<p align="center">
<img width="600" height="450" alt="锁链甲演示" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/16166f55-91a6-4ce3-b75e-52d67d90a538" />
</p>

> *No longer the weak link. Chainmail becomes the armor of tenacity.*

### 🫧 Echoes of the Deep Dark: Echo Shards *(Planned)*
Sculk is a living network. We aim to give Echo Shards the power to **sever the bond** between sculk sensors, shriekers, and the Warden—or even **deflect sonic blasts** back at their source.

> *The Warden’s own echo, turned against it.*

### 🏹 The Fletching Table’s True Purpose *(Planned)*
After years of silence, the Fletching Table finally gains a **full GUI**. Craft tipped arrows, customize arrow types, and become the archer you were meant to be.

> *Every ranger’s workstation, finally complete.*

### 🗺️ Dynamic Cartography for Structures *(Planned)*
Maps should do more than show where you’ve been. We’re developing a system where **maps dynamically hint at unexplored structures**, using subtle cartographic clues.

> *Not a minimap. A scholar’s guide to the world.*

---

## 📥 Installation

1.  Ensure you have **Fabric Loader 0.18.4+** installed for Minecraft 1.21.11.
2.  Download the latest `.jar` from the Modrinth.
3.  Place it into your `.minecraft/mods` folder.
4.  **Important:** This mod must be installed on **both the client and the server** to function correctly.

> **Currently, this mod has no required dependencies.** If this changes, they will be clearly listed here.
